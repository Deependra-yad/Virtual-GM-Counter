Governing Equations

When the pendulum is twisted by an angle \(\theta\), the wire exerts a restoring torque \(\tau\) proportional to the angle:

$$ \tau = -\kappa \theta $$

Where \(\kappa\) is the torsional constant of the wire:

$$ \kappa = \frac{\pi G r^4}{2L} $$
  • \(G\): Modulus of Rigidity of material
  • \(r\): Radius of wire
  • \(L\): Length of wire

From Newton's Second Law for Rotation (\(\tau = I\alpha\)):

$$ I \frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2} + \kappa \theta = 0 $$

This is a Simple Harmonic Motion equation. The time period \(T\) is:

$$ T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{I}{\kappa}} $$

Working Formula for Moment of Inertia:

$$ I = \frac{\kappa T^2}{4\pi^2} $$
