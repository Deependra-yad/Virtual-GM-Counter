Physics Equations

Hooke's Law

The restoring force is proportional to the extension.

$$ F_s = -k \Delta x $$

Static Equilibrium

When forces are balanced, the net force is zero.

$$ \Sigma F = m g - k \Delta x_0 = 0 $$ $$ \Delta x_0 = \frac{mg}{k} $$

Equation of Motion (SHM)

With damping $c$, applying Newton's Second Law.

$$ m\frac{d^2x}{dt^2} + c\frac{dx}{dt} + kx = 0 $$

Oscillation Properties

$$ \omega_0 = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} $$ $$ T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}} \quad , \quad f = \frac{1}{T} $$
