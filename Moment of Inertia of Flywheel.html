Governing Equations

1. Falling Mass Phase (String Attached)
Newton's 2nd Law for translating mass:
$$ mg - T = ma = m(r\alpha) $$
Rotational dynamics of flywheel:
$$ Tr - \tau_f = I\alpha $$
Solving for Angular Acceleration \(\alpha\):
$$ \alpha = \frac{mgr - \tau_f}{I + mr^2} $$
2. Coasting Phase (String Detached)
$$ \alpha = -\frac{\tau_f}{I} $$
3. Experimental Moment of Inertia (\(I\))
Using conservation of energy and work done against friction over \(N_1\) and \(N_2\) revolutions:
$$ I_{exp} = \frac{m(2gh - r^2\omega_{max}^2)}{\omega_{max}^2 \left(1 + \frac{N_1}{N_2}\right)} $$

PHYSICS LABORATORY REPORT

Experiment: Determination of the Moment of Inertia of a Flywheel

1. Aim

To determine the moment of inertia of a given flywheel about its axis of rotation and to calculate the frictional torque at the bearings.

2. Apparatus

Flywheel mounted on horizontal bearings, mass hanger, slotted masses, cotton string, digital stopwatch, vernier caliper, meter scale.

3. Formula Used

The experimental moment of inertia \(I\) is given by:

\( I = \frac{m(2gh - r^2\omega_{max}^2)}{\omega_{max}^2 \left(1 + \frac{N_1}{N_2}\right)} \)

Where:

  • \(m\) = hanging mass
  • \(h\) = height of fall (string length)
  • \(r\) = radius of the axle
  • \(\omega_{max}\) = maximum angular velocity at detachment
  • \(N_1\) = number of revolutions during fall
  • \(N_2\) = number of revolutions during coasting before stopping
