Lab Manual: Ballistic Pendulum

1. Objective

To determine the initial velocity of a projectile by applying the principles of Conservation of Linear Momentum and Conservation of Mechanical Energy using a ballistic pendulum apparatus.

2. Theory

The experiment involves a perfectly inelastic collision followed by a conservative swing. The analysis is broken into two distinct phases:

Phase 1: The Collision (Conservation of Momentum)

A projectile of mass \( m \) is fired with an unknown initial velocity \( u \) into a stationary pendulum bob of mass \( M \). The projectile embeds itself, and the combined system moves with a common velocity \( V \). Because the collision happens over a very short time, external forces (gravity, pivot tension) do negligible work during impact, so horizontal momentum is conserved:

$$m \cdot u = (M + m) \cdot V$$

Phase 2: The Swing (Conservation of Energy)

After the collision, the combined mass \( (M + m) \) swings upward. Assuming negligible friction and air resistance, the kinetic energy (KE) immediately after collision is entirely converted into gravitational potential energy (PE) at the maximum height \( h \).

$$\frac{1}{2}(M + m)V^2 = (M + m)gh$$ $$V = \sqrt{2gh}$$

Calculating Initial Velocity

The height \( h \) is determined geometrically from the length of the pendulum \( L \) and the maximum swing angle \( \theta \):

$$h = L(1 - \cos\theta)$$

Substituting \( V \) back into the momentum equation yields the final formula for the initial velocity \( u \):

$$u = \left( \frac{M + m}{m} \right) \sqrt{2gL(1 - \cos\theta)}$$

3. Procedure

  • Set the masses of the projectile ($m$) and pendulum ($M$) using the sliders in the Apparatus tab.
  • Adjust the pendulum length ($L$) and gravity ($g$).
  • Click the green FIRE PROJECTILE button to initiate the experiment.
  • Observe the perfectly inelastic collision and note the maximum angle ($\theta$) reached on the telemetry panel.
  • Navigate to the Calculations tab to review the step-by-step mathematical derivation for your specific trial.
